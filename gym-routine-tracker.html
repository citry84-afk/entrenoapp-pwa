How to use it

From your plan to a trackable session.

1

Copy your structure

For example: Push, Pull, Legs, Full Body or any split you already follow.

2

Add exercises

Include the name, target sets and whether it is tracked by weight, reps or time.

3

Log only what matters

After each exercise, save what you did. The workout should not become a long form.

4

Repeat with context

Next time, check the previous session and choose whether to hold, increase or adapt.
