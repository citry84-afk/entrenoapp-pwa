# Exercise visual assets

## EntrenoApp AI-generated illustrations

The versioned WebP files and their JPEG compatibility copies in `generated/` were created for EntrenoApp with the built-in ChatGPT image-generation tool, beginning with an original style sample approved on 2026-10-24. They are not Everkinetic images or edits of the Everkinetic PNG files below, and must not be attributed to Everkinetic.

These illustrations show static reference positions, alternative viewing angles or explicitly labelled block/equipment overviews. They are not continuous animation, measured muscle-activation maps, personalised technique assessments or professional/clinical certification. Equipment, grips, supports and obvious anatomical errors are visually checked before an image is enabled; the review does not replace qualified instruction.

The project keeps exact prompts, original generation paths, file hashes and visual-review notes in `audits/2026-10-24/generated-image-records/`, with the approved style specification in `audits/2026-10-24/generated-image-style.md`. Runtime registration is restricted to reviewed, versioned local assets.

## Selection and scope notes

- Each pair is available only to an explicitly matched exercise/material/posture. A shared app exercise ID is not sufficient. Flat and incline presses, seated and standing presses, grips, bodyweight and assisted/weighted dips, and leg-curl machine positions are not interchangeable.
- Step-up uses the source's positions 1 and 3 (ground and on the bench), not a renamed or synthesized image. The illustrated bench and dumbbells are not a low supported step for a Vital session.
- Walking-lunge positions 1 and 2 show upright and lowered positions with dumbbells. They do not demonstrate continuous travel or every alternating step.
- The original JSON references nonexistent `biceps-curls-with-barbell-*` paths for source 0211. The pinned repository tree contains the inspected originals under `bicep-curls-with-barbell-*`; the original file names above are retained.
- `dumbbell_fly_bench` is a flat-bench reference, not a floor fly. `barbell_row_supinated` shows reverse grip. `dumbbell_row_rear_delts` is the source's rear-deltoid row. Deadlift references do not replace Romanian-deadlift references.
- Wide Grip Lat Pull Down (0097), Squats with Exercise Bands (0155), cropped calf references and other mismatched posture/material illustrations remain excluded. The proposed exercise catalogue uses separately reviewed AI-generated illustrations as its first choice. An unmatched custom exercise falls back to a schematic muscle map or written cues rather than an unrelated demonstration.

File names, attribution, scope and provenance are also recorded in `assets/js/exercise-media.js`. Anatomical diagrams and the legacy movement SVGs retained for compatibility tests are separately authored schematic artwork, not copied or AI-derived versions of these illustrations. The legacy movement SVGs are not enabled in the normal exercise-guide UI.
